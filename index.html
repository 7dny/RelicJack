THE CARDWRIGHT'S GRIMOIRE

How Relicjack Works

Beginning: Open your one-time Welcome Crate to gain five random relics and 100 starting gold. Equip them in Loadout & Start.

Runs: A run contains five blackjack rounds. Before an element is revealed, choose one equipped relic. If it matches the table’s element, it resonates and becomes stronger for that round.

Relics & upgrades: The library holds 1,000 relics across 100 elemental sets. Pulling an owned relic raises its level, from I to X. A relic cannot fill two loadout slots.

Cases: The Case Forge sells 1, 3, 6, and 10-relic cases. Each relic appears through its own reel spin. Cases cost gold won at the table and through quests.

Quests: Five daily decrees refresh with your device date. Ten weekly chronicles refresh every Monday. Claim their gold rewards in the Quest Hall.
